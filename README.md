# Tata Motors FY2023–FY2025 | Business & Financial Data Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

An independent Data Analytics case study analyzing Tata Motors' publicly disclosed financial and business performance from FY2023 to FY2025.

The goal was to transform company disclosures into structured KPIs, year-over-year analysis, visualizations, and actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze consolidated financial performance
- Compare FY2023–FY2025 revenue and profitability
- Analyze Passenger Vehicle performance
- Analyze Commercial Vehicle performance
- Examine Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) performance
- Analyze margins, cash flow and business KPIs
- Identify important trends and business insights

---

## 📈 Key FY2025 Metrics

| Metric | FY2025 |
|---|---:|
| Consolidated Revenue | ₹439,695 Cr |
| EBITDA | ₹57,600 Cr |
| EBITDA Margin | 13.1% |
| PBT (BEI) | ₹34,330 Cr |
| Net Profit | ₹28,149 Cr |
| Automotive Free Cash Flow | ₹22,348 Cr |

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

### Consolidated Performance

Consolidated revenue increased from ₹345,967 Cr in FY2023 to ₹439,695 Cr in FY2025.

PBT before exceptional items increased from ₹1,803 Cr in FY2023 to ₹34,330 Cr in FY2025.

### Passenger Vehicles

FY2025 revenue was ₹48,445 Cr.

EBITDA margin improved to 6.9%.

### Commercial Vehicles

FY2025 revenue was ₹75,053 Cr.

EBITDA margin improved to 11.8%.

### Jaguar Land Rover

FY2025 revenue was approximately £28.96 billion.

EBIT margin was 8.5%.

Free cash flow was approximately £1.48 billion.
